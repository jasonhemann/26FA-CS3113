## Before Your Appointment

- Book through the link in the assignment.
- Bring the required files and checkpoints on a machine where each version
  runs.

## In the Room

Be ready to explain how your submitted code works and how you developed it.
The conversation may range across the files and course concepts named by the
assignment; there is no fixed script. It assesses understanding, not speed,
presentation polish, or memorized wording. You will receive brief feedback,
including what to revisit before a retry.
