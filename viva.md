## In the Room

A code review normally follows this sequence:

1. Open and run the requested submitted artifact or checkpoint.
2. Trace a small expression and explain how the interpreter produces its
   result.
3. Explain one relevant representation or transformation invariant: what
   changed, what did not, and why meaning is preserved.
4. Predict, modify, or diagnose a small unfamiliar case.
5. Receive a score and brief feedback.

The exact prompt varies. The goal is to demonstrate operational understanding,
not polished speaking or memorized wording.
